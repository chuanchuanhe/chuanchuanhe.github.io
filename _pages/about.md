I am a research assistant and a Ph.D. student in **Quantitative Biology and Bioinformatics** at [UNLV](https://www.unlv.edu/). I am part of the [Han Lab](https://www.unlv.edu/people/mira-han) at UNLV School of Life Sciences. 

My current research focuses on developing a multi-modal deep learning framework to predict transcription start site (TSS) activity by integrating DNA sequence, RNA-seq, and chromatin accessibility data across human cancers and plants. I have extensive experience in high-performance computing, next-generation sequencing (NGS) data analysis, bioinformatics pipeline development, and applying statistical modeling to address biological questions. 

I earned my Bachelor of Arts (BA) degree in **Computer Science** at [The University of Virginia](https://www.virginia.edu/). After that, I worked as a software engineer for four years.
